Hey, I'm Nadir.

Born in Uzbekistan, grew up in Russia, ended up in New York. At some point I stopped following the obvious path and started building something of my own.

I work in finance — helping small businesses get funded. But what I actually think about is behavior, attention, culture, and how technology shapes who we're becoming. Not as a hobby. Just the way my brain works.

On any given day you'll find me at a Brooklyn café with my laptop, at the gym at 8am, riding my Yamaha through the city at night, or on a walk with music loud enough to drown everything out.

I'm into things that are real — good conversations, the city after rain, music that stops the noise in your head, people who show up without a performance.

If that sounds like you — say hi.
